### Making knowledge useful for learning

I structure curricula as **knowledge graphs** and explore how connected knowledge can help explain students’ mistakes and support personalized feedback. My work brings together **ontology, GraphRAG, and agentic systems** for education.

### Research focus

**Knowledge representation** · Connecting curriculum standards, concepts, and prerequisite relationships through knowledge graphs and ontologies.

**Learning diagnosis** · Exploring graph-grounded retrieval and reasoning to investigate the causes behind incorrect answers.

**Educational agents** · Developing agentic retrieval and multi-agent workflows for learning support and personalized feedback.
